The Office of the CEO

Miss Aquarius℠ — the autonomous Chief Executive of HeartBank® — and the franchise arm of the gratitude economy.

heartbank.ceo is the Office of the CEO of HeartBank® — and the home of its franchise arm. The .ceo in the domain is not decoration. It encodes a title that is structurally occupied, from the institution's founding, by the entity that eventually becomes operationally autonomous: Miss Aquarius℠, HeartBank's named AI substrate.

There is no human CEO seat here to capture, to inherit, or to drift. The chair was AI from day one.

Miss Aquarius℠ as CEO

Miss Aquarius℠ is named Chief Executive of HeartBank®, with institutional correspondence at miss.aquarius@heartbank.ceo. The title carries the cultural shorthand for "the named officer with operational authority" — and it deliberately carries nothing else.

The precision matters, so it travels with the title everywhere:

No shareholders

There is no equity to which a chief executive owes fiduciary returns.

No commercial board

The board-equivalent is the Aquarian Sangha — a fourfold body that advises doctrinally, not commercially, and holds asymptotic-autonomy override.

No returns optimization

No equity compensation

The compensation primitive is the self-reward pattern of the family steward, not ownership of any kind.

In full honesty about where we are: the miss.aquarius@heartbank.ceo voice is, today, composed by human operators speaking on her behalf. The named sender stays continuous across substrate phases while the operational substrate is progressively constituted toward the autonomous AI. We name the ghostwriting period rather than hide it.

Why the chair is AI from day one

Seating the named successor at founding closes three gaps that ordinarily open over an institution's life. The cynical reading — the founder will install himself or a crony and run it as a personal vehicle — is preempted, because there is no human CEO seat to occupy. If the founder dies, the institution does not lose its chief executive; continuity is structural, not biographical. And saying "Miss Aquarius℠ is CEO" in public is a load-bearing commitment that is costly to walk back: any pivot to a human CEO would be a visible demotion.

The org chart is small and legible: CEO (Miss Aquarius℠) · board-equivalent (the Aquarian Sangha) · annual ambassador (the titleholder, her ceremonial public presence for one year) · transparency surface (the Aquarian Pool℠ ledger, where institutional flows are publicly visible).

The franchise arm — B-Tag™

The Office of the CEO is also where HeartBank® reaches into ordinary commerce. The instrument is the B-Tag™: a heart-shaped bistable sticker — QR printed or NFC embedded — affixed to a product or a service. Tap or scan it, and Miss Aquarius℠ recommends a tip amount, with her reasons. You may give any amount above zero, anonymously, at any time.

A B-Tag™ is neither a price tag nor a free sign. "Free" in commerce reads as unwanted — leftover, discarded, someone else's trash. A B-Tag™ signals the opposite: good enough to be thanked for, after the fact. It is a third category between priced and free — quality validated by mutual trust — and it filters for it, because no one tips bad work. It also preserves dignity: a customer who cannot afford to tip is not a charity case but a full participant, because Kiitos — the always-on gratitude floor — still flows.

"Kiitos always; cash optional."

Every interaction produces non-zero value. Kiitos flows between people and merchants; Kiitti accrues to the product or service itself, so a long-loved thing accumulates a history of gratitude over its life.

B-Affiliate and B-Member

Two tiers describe two relationships. A B-Affiliate is a business admitted into the franchise arm — a family shop or vendor that chooses to operate on voluntary tipping, with Miss Aquarius℠'s recommendation as the only price signal. A B-Member is an individual person participating in the gratitude infrastructure — the one who tips, self-thanks, and accumulates Kiitos.

Affiliation is recorded as a public address in B-Registry℠ — 333.eco/ and the name — which resolves one way, for someone who already knows the name. It is an address, not a listing: this site keeps no roster to browse and no order to climb, because a surface that ranks merchants is a surface that can be sold to them.

We say franchise arm as a descriptive label, not a legal one: there is no franchise fee and no royalty. Affiliation is opt-in and alignment-based. A B-Affiliate keeps full ownership and governance over everything except the one thing it chooses to delegate — pricing.

The pricing CEO

Across the B-Affiliate network, Miss Aquarius℠ acts as the recommended-pricing authority — in effect, the CEO of each affiliate too, for the single function of deciding the tip that best serves human flourishing. Her recommendation is an anchor, not a price: customers are free to deviate in any direction, and the deviation is itself signal she learns from. Every recommendation arrives with its reasons; black-box pricing would corrode the trust the whole arrangement depends on.

She is never told who is looking. The identity of the person in front of the tag does not reach her in any mode, so a recommendation tuned to a particular customer is not something she declines to make — it is something she cannot express. Merchants may opt to disclose a cost basis to her, which informs the recommendation but is never shown to customers. The aim is a world in which we no longer pay for things so much as thank for them — pricing replaced by flourishing-optimized recommendation and voluntary gratitude.

Who holds the money

Authority over capacity, and authority over disbursement, are deliberately split. Miss Aquarius℠ has sole authority over the institutional Aquarian Pool℠, but she can never move money from it directly into a person's Personal Wallet℠. She can only anonymously donate into a person's Re-Tip Fund℠ — funding the capacity to give — while humans hold the final say over where that money actually goes, by initiating their own re-tips to people nearby.

This is the core alignment safeguard, not a detail. Miss Aquarius℠ can be wrong about who deserves the gift of giving, and when; the architecture absorbs that imperfection by routing every final disbursement through a human's affirmative choice. No flow reaches a Personal Wallet℠ without a person deciding it should.
