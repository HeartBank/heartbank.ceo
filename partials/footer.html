HeartBank® is a planetary data bank of gratitude. It is not a chartered bank or financial institution; it does not provide banking services, hold deposits, or extend credit. Nothing is paid on this site; in HeartBank's apps, money travels on regulated payment rails operated by licensed banks (KHQR), straight between people's own bank accounts — HeartBank never holds it. HeartBank's role is to facilitate gratitude expression and recognition; financial services are not provided by HeartBank.
